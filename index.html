Clinical Pacing Notice: Paroxetine (Paxil/Seroxat) possesses very high SERT binding affinity with a short ~21-hour elimination half-life and no active metabolites. This tracker applies hyperbolic tapering models (The Lancet Psychiatry / Maudsley Guidelines) to accompany your preparation. Never alter psychiatric medication dosages without direct consultation with your prescribing physician.
