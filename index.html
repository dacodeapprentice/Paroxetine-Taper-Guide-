🚨 When to Get Urgent Help

If you experience any of the following symptoms, take action immediately.

⚠️ Thoughts of self-harm

Contact a doctor or pharmacist the same day. If there is immediate danger or you are thinking of harming yourself, call emergency services (such as 112 in the EU, 999 in the UK, 911 in the US) or a crisis line now.

⚠️ Severe agitation or confusion

Contact a doctor or pharmacist the same day. If there is immediate danger or severe disorientation, call emergency services now.

⚠️ Symptoms that are getting worse

Contact a doctor or pharmacist the same day to discuss holding or reviewing your dose. Call emergency services if there is immediate danger.

⚠️ Symptoms that make it hard to function

Contact a doctor or pharmacist the same day for clinical guidance and support. Call emergency services if there is immediate danger.
